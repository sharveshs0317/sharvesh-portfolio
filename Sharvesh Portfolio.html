01 / ABOUT

Building toward the cloud.

I am Sharvesh S, a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Lovely Professional University .

My interests span cloud computing, DevOps, Kubernetes, Linux, cybersecurity, software engineering and embedded systems.

I enjoy turning ideas into practical technical systems — from web applications and data-driven tools to cloud infrastructure and sensor-based embedded prototypes.

My approach is hands-on and practical: understand the problem, break it into clear steps, build a working version, and improve it through testing and feedback. I value clean logic, dependable systems, and interfaces that are easy for people to use.

At this stage, I am strengthening my foundations in data structures, cloud infrastructure, DevOps workflows, Kubernetes, Linux and cybersecurity while continuing to build projects that connect these areas.

I also enjoy working across different layers of technology. Building web experiences has helped me understand user-facing development, while projects involving data, cloud concepts and embedded hardware have expanded the way I think about systems as a whole.

Beyond learning individual technologies, I focus on developing an engineering mindset: writing maintainable code, understanding how components communicate, troubleshooting problems systematically and learning from every iteration. My goal is to keep moving from project-based learning toward designing reliable, scalable solutions.
